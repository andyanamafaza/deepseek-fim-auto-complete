### Verify the connection

Run **DeepSeek Autocomplete: Test Connection** to confirm your key is accepted
and the API is reachable. The exact reason is shown in the notification itself -
an invalid key, no remaining credits, a rate limit, or a network/timeout error.

For the full request log, [open the DeepSeek log](command:deepseekFim.showLog),
or click the status bar item and choose **Show Log**.
